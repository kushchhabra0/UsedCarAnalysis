Analytical Methodology & Discovery Narrative

Rigorous ML pipeline transferring account segmentation methodology to automotive marketplace data.
01
DATA HYGIENE

Informative Missingness (MNAR Prospect Structure)

Rather than discarding listings missing specs (e.g. 71.7% missing vehicle size, 41.6% cylinders), missing values were explicitly tagged with structural 'NP' (Not Provided) tokens. This preserves non-profiling behavior as a predictive market signal rather than corrupting distributions.

26.17% of listings fall into the Under-Profiled Bargain segment due to missing specification details.
02
MODEL STABILITY

Density Seeding Fix (Cao vs. Huang)

Standard init='Huang' uses stochastic mode sampling, yielding low seed stability (ARI ~ 0.38) on high-cardinality nominal data. Switching to deterministic Cao density-based centroid seeding stabilized cluster bounds, raising seed ARI to 0.9997.

0.9997 ARI achieved across 5 random initialization seeds (> 0.85 benchmark threshold).
03
FEATURE SELECTION

Cramér's V Association Screening

Screened nominal categorical pairs to eliminate collinearity distortion. Discovered moderate redundant alignment between vehicle type and drive ($V=0.48$) and manufacturer vs type ($V=0.38$).

V = 0.48 association detected between drive type and vehicle category.
