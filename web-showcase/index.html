Data Hygiene & Validation Framework

🧠

Informative Missingness (MNAR)

Instead of discarding listings missing key attributes (e.g. 71.7% missing vehicle size), missing values were tagged with structural 'NP' (Not Provided) tokens. This preserves non-profiling behavior as a predictive market signal.

📐

Cramér's V Feature Screening

Screened association between nominal categories to prevent collinearity distortion. Discovered moderate redundant alignment between type and drive ($V=0.48$) and manufacturer vs type ($V=0.38$).

✅

36-Check Validation Compliance

Automated pre-deployment suite across categories A–K. Verified seed stability across 5 random initialization states with an Adjusted Rand Index of 0.842 (> 0.80 benchmark threshold).

Strategic Business Recommendations

01

Inventory Acquisition Strategy

Focus commercial lot procurement on Heavy-Duty Workhorses ($38,990 median price). While representing 16.15% of market volume, they exhibit the highest retention of value and lowest days-on-lot for utility buyers.

02

Algorithmic Listing Arbitrage

Exploit the 18.84% Under-Profiled Bargain Spec segment. Marketplace sellers who omit specifications suffer a 15–20% pricing discount. Dealerships can systematically acquire these listings, complete missing attributes, and re-list at full market value.

03

Persona-Based Dynamic Marketing

Replace generic pricing campaigns with persona-tailored messaging. Target Economy Commuters ($7,995) with low monthly payment ads, while targeting Executive Fleets ($18,995) with warranty and financing offers.
