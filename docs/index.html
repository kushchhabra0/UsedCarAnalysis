Data Hygiene & Validation Framework

🧠

Informative Missingness (MNAR)

Instead of discarding listings missing key attributes (e.g. 71.7% missing vehicle size), missing values were tagged with structural 'NP' (Not Provided) tokens. This preserves non-profiling behavior as a predictive market signal.

📐

Cao Density Seeding Fix

Replaced stochastic Huang initialization with deterministic Cao density-based centroid seeding, resolving initial mode instability and boosting seed ARI from 0.3842 to 0.9997.

✅

36-Check Validation Compliance

Automated pre-deployment suite across categories A–K. Verified seed stability across 5 random initialization states with an Adjusted Rand Index of 0.9997 (> 0.85 benchmark threshold).

Strategic Business Recommendations

01

Inventory Acquisition Strategy

Focus commercial lot procurement on Heavy-Duty Workhorses ($43,900 median price). While representing 10.84% of market volume, they exhibit the highest retention of value and lowest days-on-lot for utility buyers.

02

Algorithmic Listing Arbitrage

Exploit the 26.17% Under-Profiled Bargain Spec segment. Marketplace sellers who omit specifications suffer a 15–20% pricing discount. Dealerships can systematically acquire these listings, complete missing attributes, and re-list at full market value.

03

Persona-Based Dynamic Marketing

Replace generic pricing campaigns with persona-tailored messaging. Target Economy Commuters ($5,500) with low monthly payment ads, while targeting Executive Fleets ($8,900) with warranty and financing offers.
